General instructions

  1. The clock has been set at the server and the countdown at the top of the screen will display the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the test will be submitted automatically.
  2. The Question Palette shows the status of every question using the colours explained below. Use it to jump directly to any question. On phones, tap the 📋 Palette button at the top right to open and close it.
  3. Each correct answer awards +4 marks; each incorrect answer deducts −1 mark. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks.
  4. To select an option, click on the option text or its radio button. To change your answer, simply click a different option.
  5. Clicking Save & Next saves your answer to the current question and moves to the next one. Clicking Clear Response removes your selected answer.
  6. Clicking Mark for Review & Next flags the question for revisiting later. A marked question that also has a saved answer will still be evaluated.
  7. Clicking the plain << Back / Next >> buttons at the bottom moves between questions without saving your current selection.
  8. You can navigate between subjects at any time using the tabs at the top.
