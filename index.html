General instructions

  1. The clock has been set at the server and the countdown at the top of the screen will display the remaining time. When the timer reaches zero, the test will be submitted automatically.
  2. The Question Palette on the right shows the status of every question using the colours explained below. Use it to jump directly to any question.
  3. Each correct answer awards +4 marks; each incorrect answer deducts −1 mark. Unattempted questions receive 0 marks. (Standard JEE Main scheme — adjust in your own head if your paper says otherwise.)
  4. To select an option, click on the option text or its radio button. To change your answer, simply click a different option — no need to clear it first.
  5. Clicking Save & Next saves your answer to the current question and moves to the next one. Clicking Clear Response removes your selected answer for the current question.
  6. Clicking Mark for Review & Next flags the question for revisiting later. A marked question that also has a saved answer will still be evaluated.
  7. Clicking the plain << Back / Next >> buttons at the bottom moves between questions without saving your current selection — get in the habit of using Save & Next instead.
  8. You can navigate between Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (or whatever sections your paper has) at any time using the tabs at the top.
  9. Do not attempt to close this tab or exit full‑screen during the test — the timer keeps running regardless.
